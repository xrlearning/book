# XREN Book

This book presents the main results of the XREN (eXtended Reality tools to support learning activities in ENgineering) [project](https://www.xr-en.eu/) (n. 2023-1-EE01-KA220-HED-000158491) funded by the European Commission’s [Erasmus+ programme](https://erasmus-plus.ec.europa.eu/projects) in the period from September 2023 to August 2026.

This introduction gives an overview of the project [consortium](#xren-consortium), [activities](#xren-activities), and [results](#xren-results).


## XREN Consortium 

The XREN project involved four partners:
* **Tallinn University of Technology** (TalTech), Department of Mechanical and Industrial Engineering ([DMIE](https://taltech.ee/en/department-mechanical-and-industrial-engineering)) 
* **Politecnico di Milano** (Polimi), Department of Mechanical Engineering ([DMEC](https://www.mecc.polimi.it/))
* **Consiglio Nazionale delle Ricerche** (CNR), Institute of Intelligent Industrial Technologies and Systems for Advanced Manufacturing ([STIIMA](https://www.stiima.cnr.it/?lang=en))
* **University of Belgrade** (UBelg), Faculty of Mechanical Engineering ([FME](http://arhiva.rect.bg.ac.rs/en/members/faculties/FME.php))


## XREN Activities

The main objective of XREN was to **strengthen the digital competences of engineering students**, with particular emphasis on extended reality (XR) technologies, while supporting the integration of XR into higher education courses in engineering.

To achieve this objective, the project developed a set of reusable learning materials organised as **XR-based learning workflows**. Each workflow specifies the learning objectives, use cases, tasks, user-experience and interaction modalities, and supporting technologies. The workflows are accompanied by relevant data, digital content, and prototype tools that support their implementation in the classroom and laboratory. These resources also facilitate collaboration among university laboratories within and beyond the XREN consortium, enabling students to participate in learning activities or access laboratory resources remotely through virtual environments.

The project aimed to develop **10 XR-based learning workflows** for industrial engineering education, supported by reference documentation and practical implementation resources. During the project, the workflows were integrated into the participating higher education institutions and tested across **three curricula and six Bachelor’s and Master’s degree courses**, delivered through both traditional and blended learning models.

The project activities were organised into four work packages:

- **WP1 – Project Management:** coordination of the project, monitoring of progress, quality assurance, and administrative and financial management.
- **WP2 – XR Workflows for Engineering Education:** development and implementation of XR-based learning workflows in real higher education settings within industrial and mechanical engineering.
- **WP3 – XR Learning Co-design Labs and Workshops:** organisation of long-term co-design laboratories in which engineering students used, tested, and contributed to the development of the XR learning workflows. WP3 also included intensive XR learning workshops that engaged students in immersive, collaborative, and practice-oriented learning experiences.
- **WP4 – Dissemination of Tools and Workflows:** communication and dissemination of the project results, including the developed tools, learning workflows, educational materials, and implementation experiences.


## XREN Results

The implementation of **WP2** resulted in the following outputs:

| Indicator | Result |
|:----------|------:|
| XR learning workflows developed | **11** |
| Industrial use cases modelled and adapted for XR workflows | **5** |
| Higher education curricula enhanced through the developed workflows | **3** |
| Engineering courses integrating the workflows by the end of the project | **6** |
| Higher education courses expected to integrate the workflows during the follow-up phase | **12** |

The implementation of **WP3** resulted in the following outputs:

| Indicator | Result |
|:----------|------:|
| Co-design activities conducted | **12** |
| Students involved in co-design activities | **64** |
| Online meetings involving students from partner universities | **12** |
| XR learning workshops conducted | **4** |
| Students participating in the workshops | **78** |

Each partner university carried out four student project activities during the three-year project period, resulting in a total of 12 co-design activities.

The implementation of **WP4** resulted in the following outputs:

| Indicator | Result |
|:----------|------:|
| Repository for publishing tools and workflows | **1** |
| Project-specific online channels | **2** |
| Articles published or accepted for publication | **6** |
